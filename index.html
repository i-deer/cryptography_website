Your Security Starts Here

Cryptography is a big topic. Cybersecurity is even bigger.

The point of this blog website is to educate the everyday person (Yeah I'm looking at you) about the basics of cryptography and how these are relevant to their everyday life.

It is also to push those cryptography enthusiasts (Yes I am still looking at you) to meet other people, share knowledge with each other and narrow the divide between cyber and everyday people

because at the end of the day, cyber is for the people. Cyber is run by the people. Cyber needs the people.

ENJOY :)
